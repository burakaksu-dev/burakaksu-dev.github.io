How do I play?

Drag any row left or right, or any column up or down; the whole line slides and wraps around the edge. Seat the cats so that every room has exactly one cat. In later levels each row and column also needs exactly one cat, and no two cats may touch, not even diagonally. A worried or angry cat tells you what is wrong: tap it to see why. Padlocked lines cannot slide.

Hints, moves and stars

Each level has a move limit. Solve it in the fewest moves for three stars. Hints show the next best slide; you get a free hint every day, and when you run out you can watch an optional ad for another. If you run out of moves, you can watch an ad for +5 moves or try again.

Fish and the inn

Finishing a level for the first time, earning new stars, opening a floor chest and solving the daily puzzle give you fish. Spend fish in My Inn to furnish the room where your cats live. Replaying a level you already finished gives fish only for new stars.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your levels, stars, fish, furniture and even an unfinished game are saved on your device automatically. Progress is not stored online, so deleting the app or changing phones starts a new game.

I bought "Remove ads". How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account, open the game and tap Settings → Restore purchases. You will not be charged again. Refunds are handled by the stores: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history for Google Play.

How do I turn off reminders, sound or vibration?

Open the game, tap the settings icon on the main screen, and use the switches. You can also turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: Settings → Ad privacy settings in the game. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: your device's Ads settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Does Pati Pansiyon work offline?

Yes, every level and the daily puzzle work without an internet connection. Ads are shown only when you are online; if no ad is available, rewards are given for free.
