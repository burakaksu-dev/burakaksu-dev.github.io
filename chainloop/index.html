Chainloop

Place · match · chain

A relaxing offline puzzle game. Drag pieces onto a winding path, match three of a color and watch the gap close into chains. 500 levels in 25 worlds, ice, rocks, Jokers and Bombs, 10 themes, a daily puzzle and an endless classic mode.
