Last Lighthouse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Last Lighthouse is a single-player game. We do not ask for an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (pearls, upgrades, records, missions, light colors) and your settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic information. On iOS you are asked for permission before the advertising identifier is used; on Android you can reset or disable it in your device settings. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in the game's Settings › Ad privacy settings.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

"Remove ads" is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
